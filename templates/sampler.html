🔍 Physics-grounded generative design of inherently stable, novel and controllable crystal structures

Song, Z., Zhou, Q., Ling, C., Li, Q., Cheng, L., Wang, J. (2026). arXiv:2507.19307

A physics-grounded generative framework for the inverse design of crystal structures, targeting the generation of candidates that are inherently stable, novel, and controllable. The approach combines generative sampling over crystal chemical space with physical constraints to guide the search toward synthesizable materials.
