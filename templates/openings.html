Our PhD and MPhil positions are full, and we are not recruiting for any upcoming admission cycle. We do not expect PhD or MPhil recruitment to resume before Fall 2029. We are also not recruiting research assistants (RAs) or postdoctoral researchers at present.

A personal note. Our research involves difficult problems that need sustained attention and careful, hands-on work. I still write much of the code myself, alongside supervising our current projects. That leaves limited time to train and mentor new people. I have therefore decided to keep the group small and focus on supporting the people already here.

We receive many thoughtful applications from strong candidates, and turning people away is the hardest part of this decision. If you have taken the time to write to us, thank you for your interest and effort. Our limited capacity is not a judgment of your ability. I hope you find a group where you can pursue your ideas with the time and support you need.

Our group is affiliated only with HKUST's Department of Chemistry and Department of Chemical and Biological Engineering (CBE). When PhD and MPhil recruitment resumes, admission will be through Chemistry or CBE. We are not affiliated with CSE, ECE, or Physics.

HKUST undergraduate and master's students may still undertake research-credit projects with us through UROP, FYP, or master's research projects. The HKUST UROP, FYP, & Master Research page lists our current active projects.

If your research interests are closely aligned with ours, you may still write to us with any of the materials below. This does not change the recruitment status above, and we may not be able to reply to every message.

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Academic transcript
  • Research proposal
  • Representative publications or other research outputs
  • Expected starting date
