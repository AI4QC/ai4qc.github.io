Unfortunately, we are not able to take on new PhD or MPhil students in the coming admission cycles, and we do not expect this to change before Fall 2029. We are also not able to take on research assistants (RAs) or postdoctoral researchers at present. We are sorry that this also covers Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS) and self-funded applications. We will update this page if the situation changes.

A personal note. This was a difficult decision for me. The reason is my own limited capacity, and it is no reflection on the many excellent students who write to us. I still write much of the code and work closely on each project, and training someone well takes a lot of time. For now, I want the students already in the group to get the support they deserve, so I have chosen to keep the group small. I would rather be honest about this than take on more students than I can support well.

Thank you sincerely to everyone who has taken the time to write to us. Your interest means a lot to us, and I am sorry that I cannot offer more at this time. I hope you find a group where you can pursue your ideas with the time and support you need. If you would like to know more about our work, our recent publications are a good place to start.

Our group is affiliated with HKUST's Department of Chemistry and Department of Chemical and Biological Engineering (CBE). When PhD and MPhil recruitment resumes, admission will be through one of these two departments.

If your interests are closely aligned with ours, you are welcome to send us any of the materials below. Please understand that this does not change the status above. We may not be able to reply to every message, and we are sorry in advance if we do not get back to you.

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • A short note on your research interests
  • Representative publications or other research outputs, if you have any
